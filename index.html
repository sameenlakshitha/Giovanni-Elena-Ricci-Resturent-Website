Our Story

We Invite You to Visit Our Italian Restaurant

Founded in 2012, Giovanni & Elena Ricci began as a small corner trattoria with a dream: to serve food that brings people together. Today, we're proud to welcome guests every evening with the same passion for authentic Italian flavours that started it all. Every dish tells a story of our family, our heritage, and our love for la dolce vita.
